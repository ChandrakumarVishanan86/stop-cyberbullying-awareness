Cyberbullying Facts & Statistics

Home Quiz Resources Facts
  • About 37% of young people between the ages of 12 and 17 have been bullied online.
  • 1 in 3 young people have experienced cyberbullying at some point in their lives.
  • Girls are more likely to be victims of cyberbullying than boys.
  • Cyberbullying can lead to anxiety, depression, and low self-esteem.
  • Reporting cyberbullying increases the chances of it stopping.
  • Cyberbullying often goes unreported due to fear or shame.
  • Most cyberbullying happens on social media apps.
  • Some bullies don't understand the impact of their actions.
