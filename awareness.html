Cyberbullying Awareness & Prevention

1. What is Cyberbullying?

Cyberbullying is the use of electronic communication to bully a person, typically by sending messages of an intimidating or threatening nature through social media, texting, or email.

2. Real Stories and Witness Reports

Many victims have experienced emotional distress, isolation, and fear due to online bullying. Sharing real-life stories helps create awareness and promote empathy.

3. How to Support Victims

If you see someone being bullied online, report it, support the victim, and never encourage or share the harmful content.

4. How to Prevent Cyberbullying

Use privacy settings, avoid sharing personal details, be kind online, and educate others about the harm caused by cyberbullying.
