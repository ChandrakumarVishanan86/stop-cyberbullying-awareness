⚙️_What is Cyberbullying?

📱 Cyberbullying is bullying that takes place over digital devices like cell phones, computers, and tablets. It can include sending, posting, or sharing negative, harmful, false, or mean content about someone else.


⚙️_Why Stop Cyberbullying?

  • 🧠 Protect mental health
  • 🌍 Create a safe online environment
  • 🤝 Encourage respectful communication
  • 💬Promote digital citizenship

⚙️_What Can You Do?

  1. 💡 Think before you post
  2. 🚨 Report cyberbullying
  3. 🤗 Support victims
  4. 🌈 Be kind online

🚫 Let's Stand Against Cyberbullying 🚫

  1. 📱 Every click matters. Every word counts. 💬
  2. 💖 Be kind, be respectful, be safe online.
  3. 🙌 Together, we can make the internet a better place.
